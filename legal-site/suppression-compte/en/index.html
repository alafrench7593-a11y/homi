From the app

Profile › My account › Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Without the app

Write to support@homi.invalid from your account’s email address, with the subject “Account deletion”. We reply only to that address, to check that it is really you, and the deletion is carried out within one month at most. You receive a confirmation.

What is erased

Your account, your entries, your photos and your documents. If your household is shared, the household data stays with the co-parent. For a “Sign in with Apple” account, the app’s access to your Apple ID is also revoked.

What is not erased

Invoices, kept by Apple or Google as required by law. Deleting the account does not stop the subscription: cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings.
