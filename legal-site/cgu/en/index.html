Purpose

HOMI is an app that supports parents, from the wish for a child through to family life. It helps you record, organise and find information. It never replaces the advice of a health professional.

Minimum age

The app is for people aged 18 or over.

Account

You are responsible for keeping your login details confidential. You can delete your account at any time from Profile › My account: your data and photos are then erased.

Health information

The content and the assistant provide general, sourced and cautious information. They do not make diagnoses, prescribe or calculate doses. In an emergency, call 15 or 112.

Community

Exchanges must remain kind and respectful. Messages that are against the law or these rules may be removed and their authors suspended.

Subscription

Some features are reserved for the Premium subscription, managed by the App Store or Google Play. You cancel from your store’s settings. Safety information always remains available free of charge.

Price, trial and renewal

HOMI Premium comes in three plans that give exactly the same access: monthly (€10.99 per month), 6 months (€49.99 every 6 months) and 12 months (€79.99 per year), indicative prices in France on the date of these terms. The price that applies is the one shown in the app as supplied by your store (App Store or Google Play), taxes included, and confirmed by the store before any payment. A 3-day free trial is offered only on the monthly plan, and only if your store indicates that you are eligible (usually once); otherwise payment starts as soon as you subscribe. No trial is offered on the 6-month and 12-month plans. The subscription renews automatically for the same period, unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, from your Apple or Google account settings; access stays open until the end of the paid period. Changing plan replaces the current subscription (never two subscriptions); a shorter plan takes effect at the renewal date. Any refund is decided by the store under its own rules; a refunded subscription ends Premium access immediately.

Family sharing

A Premium subscription benefits the subscriber and one other adult co-parent in their household (two adult accounts at most). Carers (nanny, grandparents…) do not benefit from it.

Deleting your account ≠ cancelling

Deleting your HOMI account erases your data but does not stop payment: cancellation is always done in your store’s settings. The “Manage my subscription” button (Profile › My subscription) takes you there directly.

Children’s data

Children do not have accounts. Information and photos about them are entered by a parent or an authorised person in the household, who remains responsible for it towards the other holders of parental authority.

Liability

The publisher does its best to provide reliable and up-to-date information, but cannot guarantee that it is free of errors. Always check the instructions on packaging, leaflets and from your health professional.

Governing law

French law applies. In the event of a dispute, an amicable solution will be sought before any legal action.
