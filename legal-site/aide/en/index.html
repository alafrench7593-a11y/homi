Write to us

A question, a problem, a suggestion: support@homi.invalid. You can also use “Give feedback” in Profile › Settings.

Emergency

The team cannot respond to emergencies. In France, call 15 (SAMU) or 112; in distress or with suicidal thoughts, 3114, free, 24/7.

Subscription

Subscribing, cancelling and refunds are handled in your Apple or Google account settings. After changing phone: Profile › My subscription › Restore purchases.

Your data

Viewing, export and deletion: Profile › Privacy. To exercise your rights by email: support@homi.invalid.
