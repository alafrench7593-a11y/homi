Data controller

David PRINCIVIL, publisher of HOMI, as a private individual. For any question or to exercise your rights, write to support@homi.invalid: you will get a reply within one month at most.

Legal basis

Health data (cycle, pregnancy, children’s health, mood, intercourse) is processed only with your explicit consent, recorded and dated, which you can withdraw at any time in Profile › Privacy. The account, the subscription and the security of the service rely on performance of the contract; the keeping of invoices (by Apple or Google) relies on a legal obligation.

Data collected

Account (email), profile, information you enter (pregnancy, children, tracking, cycle), time-stamped consents. Health data is processed only with your explicit consent.

Purposes

To provide the app’s features, your reminders, sharing with the members of your household according to the rights you choose, and the security of the service. Never targeted advertising, never resale of your data.

Who can see your data

You, and the members of your household according to the rights you give them, area by area. Your personal data (mood, cycle, weight) is shared only if you choose. In the community, only your pseudonym is visible. In demo mode, everything stays on your device.

Hosting

Database hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland, Amazon Web Services servers in the eu-west-1 region). Files and accounts are hosted in the same place. Secret keys stay on the server.

Artificial intelligence

Some features use Google Gemini artificial intelligence (paid API tier), only if you turn them on or run them: the assistant (your questions, without name or email), analysis of photos you choose to send, automatic reading of a document you choose, explanation of a scanned product, and, in the community, the summary or translation of a discussion (the public messages of the discussion are then sent). The data is sent from our servers for that single response only. Neither HOMI nor Google uses it to train an AI; however, Google may keep it for up to 55 days to detect abuse, with possible human review if it is flagged. The conversational assistant is currently paused (“coming soon”): no question is sent to it until it reopens.

Sensitive photos

Test photos are analysed on your phone and deleted by default. If you choose to keep them, they are encrypted on the phone before being saved.

Children’s photo album

Photos added to a child’s album are reduced in size on your phone and saved without their metadata (including GPS location), in a private space that is never public. They can be seen only by the members of the household who have access to that child’s profile, or by you alone if you choose “only me”. They are displayed through temporary links (1 hour). They are not sent to any artificial intelligence service.

Services used (processors)

Supabase: hosting of the database, files and accounts. Google (Gemini API): assistant replies and photo analyses that you request. Google (Places): searching for places around you, only when you run it (location sent for that search). Apple and Google: payment for the subscription. RevenueCat: subscription tracking (account identifier and purchases, no health data). Expo: delivery of notifications (neutral text, no health detail). Resend: sign-in emails. Sentry: crash reports, only if you accept them, without personal data. Open Food Facts and RappelConso: only the scanned barcode is sent. OpenStreetMap (Overpass servers, including a third-party mirror, maps and Wikimedia Commons photos): when you search for places near you without Google, your phone contacts these services directly, and they receive your IP address and an approximate location. Several of these companies are based in the United States: transfers of data outside the European Union rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified, otherwise on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Notifications and lock screen

Notifications show no medicine name, no dose, no cycle or health detail, and no community pseudonym: the detail is shown only once the app is open.

Security

Row-by-row access control on the server (each person sees only their own data and the household data that has been opened to them), secret keys only on the server side, intimate data never kept in the phone’s cache, encrypted test photos, anonymous posts that are genuinely anonymous, optional Face ID or passcode lock.

Retention period

For as long as your account exists. You can delete each entry at any time and set a preferred retention period in Profile › Privacy. Deleting the account permanently erases your data and files, except what the law requires us to keep (billing, handled by Apple or Google).

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, portability (JSON and PDF export in the app), objection and withdrawal of consent at any time. You can complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.
